# OmniEnergy Lab 3D — Energy Lab (Fink)

Interactive 3D STEM Science Laboratory built with **Three.js (WebGL2)** and **Web Audio API**, aligned to **Florida 5th Grade NGSSS Science Standards**:

- **SC.5.P.10.1**: Investigate and describe basic forms of energy (Light, Heat/Thermal, Sound, Electrical, Chemical, Mechanical).
- **SC.5.P.10.2**: Investigate and explain that energy has the ability to cause motion or create change.
- **SC.5.P.10.4**: Investigate and explain energy transformations (electrical to light, heat, sound, and motion; chemical to mechanical and electrical).

## 6 Interactive 3D Lab Stations
1. **Sound Energy**: Tuning Fork & Ping-Pong Ball (both direct contact kick & air-gap sound-wave motion) + Deep-Bass Subwoofer Speaker Cone & Bouncing Particles
2. **Light Energy**: 4-Mirror Laser Reflection Puzzle & 0° Straight-Back Bounce Demo + Beaker/Pencil & Optical Prism Rainbow Dispersion + Shadow Lab with 4 Clickable Shapes (Block, Star, Circle, Silly Puppet) & Side-Panel Material Controls
3. **Thermal Energy**: 3D Molecular Motion Phase Chamber with Continuous Slider & Water Benchmark Presets (`0°C/32°F` Solid, `24°C/75°F` Liquid, `100°C/212°F` Gas) + Chrome Kitchen Toaster Demo
4. **Mechanical Energy**: Draggable Starting-Height Lift Hill Roller Coaster with `0`, `1`, or `2` Loop Challenge + Mass/Height Inclined Ramp 6-Pin Scatter Test
5. **Chemical Energy**: Cyclist Tire-Driven Copper Dynamo Generator powering *Sunshine Bay, FL* House Windows & 8 Streetlights + Cutaway 4-Cylinder Combustion Engine
6. **Electrical Energy**: Store-Bought Modular Snap-Circuits® Style 7×5 Pegboard Kit (starts empty — freely snap Battery, 2-Snap & 3-Snap Wires, Switch, White/Red/Blue Lights, Motor Fan, and Alarm Siren in series or parallel)

## Netlify Deployment
This repository includes a preconfigured `netlify.toml` (`npm run build` → publishes `dist/`) as well as a prebuilt standalone `dist/index.html` with zero external asset dependencies.
